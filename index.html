💡 Smart Unban Tips

  • 1 appeal per week — baar-baar appeal mat karo, response 7–10 din tak aata hai.
  • In-app appeal sabse fast — disabled notice par "Appeal" button dikhe to wahi se karo.
  • ID / selfie ready — Instagram government ID ya handwritten code wali selfie maang sakta hai.
  • Spam folder check — Instagram ka response email spam mein bhi ja sakta hai.
  • Hacked case: pehle instagram.com/hacked se recovery, appeal baad mein.
